Support

Something not working, a place that's wrong, or an idea? Email support@theslowroad.app. I read every message myself.

What helps me help you

  • What you were doing, and what happened instead of what you expected.
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About).
  • The app's version (More → About).
  • The place, road or page, if it's about one.
  • A screenshot, if you can take one.

If something on a page is wrong, like a road that's closed, a name that's off, or a fact you know better, tell me where and what. Corrections are welcome.

Please don't send passwords or anything else private. I'll never ask for them.
